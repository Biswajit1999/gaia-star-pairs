# 2026-11-12 independent uncertainty audit

Research scope: [issue #1](https://github.com/Biswajit1999/gaia-star-pairs/issues/1). Projected astrometric kinematics use a common positive parallax and a hypothetical total mass; the escape-speed expression is a conditional ceiling at projected separation, not evidence of gravitational binding. Delta-method covariance omits cross-correlation with parallax. Monte Carlo rejects nonphysical negative parallax draws rather than silently clipping.

All numerical tests use synthetic inputs. No observational claim, archive access, or alteration to retained data is made. This extension was implemented with AI coding assistance. Biswajit Jana remains the requested sole authenticated Git author; no co-authors are claimed.

Reproduce: `python -m pytest -q research/test_gaia_pair_uncertainty_20261112.py`. Dependencies: Python 3, numpy, pytest.
